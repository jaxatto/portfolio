# Portfolio landscape: titles, heroes and what stands out

Reference for the hero, role-line and "is this too samey?" decisions (D-016, D-017). Captured 2026-10-13. This is a snapshot of first-load heroes at about 1200px wide in Chrome; not a ranking, and not exhaustive.

## How it was collected

- **Pass 1 (text):** 21 personal sites fetched as page text. 16 returned content; 5 did not (3 domains did not resolve, 2 redirected to unrelated pages and were not followed). Text extraction cannot see styling, so "no special treatment" is weak evidence for these.
- **Pass 2 (visual):** 15 sites screenshotted (the first top-of-page views). Two earlier ones (Adham Dannaway, Lochie Axon) were also checked visually.
- Sources used to find them: ADPList, UXPin, a GitHub portfolio-ideas list, web search. Aggregator pages are thin; verify a site before relying on it.

## What the labels say

- **"Design Engineer"** is the common term for the hybrid role: 8 of the sites above use it (Emil, Lochie, Samuel, Julien, Rick, Rafael, Porter, Jhey in his bio). Several pair it with a company name (Family, Raycast, Rootline, Retro, Shopify).
- **"Design Technologist"** is used by none of them. It appears in this site's footer and in some job postings, but in this sample it is not how people label themselves.
- **"Product Designer" plus code** is the other pattern: "+ developer", "& front-end developer" (Adham, Ruben, Peter, Darnes, Dennis).
- **Engineer-first labels** ("Frontend Engineer", "Senior product engineer") are used by people who lead with code (Brittany, Jemima, Danny).
- **Level words** ("Staff", "Senior") are rare in labels. Only Danny Garcia ("Senior") and Benji Taylor ("Head of") show level; most rely on a company name or years of experience.

## What the heroes do

- **Plain text** is common (Tait, Dejan, Waalders, Jemima, George Francis): a title, a sentence, then work. Being on this end is fine; it reads as confident.
- **Memorable ones put the personality in visuals:** 3D (Danny, Maxime), huge or animated type (Dennis, Adrien, Jemima), stickers (Deidre), an image treatment (Aaron, Rafael).
- **Duality is usually stated in a label or split layout** (Adham's designer / <coder>), not inside the sentence.

## The "design" selection box + "build" cursor idea (D-017)

- Searched the sites above and the web; no site found that pairs a design-tool selection box on one verb with a terminal cursor on another inside a greeting.
- Neither motif is new on its own (Figma-style selection handles are common design-tool UI; code chips and block cursors are common developer visuals).
- Closest ideas: Adham Dannaway's designer / <coder> split, Adrien Gervaix's inline photo pill in a headline, Rafael Conde's faded name.
- Not covered: Dribbble and Behance, and anything newer than this snapshot.

## Implications for the title

- Keep **Staff Product Designer** as the verifiable level and role; add design-engineering signals through evidence (this site is built in code, GitHub link, the "design and build" sentence).
- If a design-engineering label is wanted in the footer or meta, **"Design Engineer"** matches how the field labels itself better than "Design Technologist".
- Only use a label the resume and LinkedIn also support; mismatches read worse than a modest title.
